# Rights and licensing status

**Author: Prasuna Chatla.**

This package does not select a new reuse license for the manuscript, original diagrams, or code on the author's behalf. A public hosting decision is separate from a decision to grant reuse permissions. Before offering the project under an open license, the author should select the intended terms and verify the rights applicable to every included component.

The paper attributes primary studies underlying reported measurements and explanatory redraws. A citation is not itself a blanket license to redistribute source-paper content. Original third-party paper PDFs are not bundled. The numerical data and redraw provenance are documented in `docs/EVIDENCE.md`.

The bundled CSL style has its own existing license, preserved in its header and recorded in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. That license does not automatically apply to the rest of this repository. No font files or IEEE template distribution are included; obtain compilation dependencies from their normal distributions.
