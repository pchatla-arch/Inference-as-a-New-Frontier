Inference as a New Frontier

A Data-Movement-Centric Analysis of Large-Model Serving and Reinforcement Learning Rollouts

Prasuna Chatla · Independent Researcher

Read the paperEditable manuscriptBibTeX citation

The systems question

How much useful work is extracted from every byte moved? The paper connects KV caches, paged attention, continuous batching, speculation, quantization, phase disaggregation, MoE serving, and RL rollouts through a shared cost model.

Beyond generation throughput

The updated analysis treats judging, checking, repair, tools, and training as parts of an improvement campaign. It proposes accounting for bytes per verified improvement and externally assessed quality gain per joule—not just tokens per second.

Evidence and reproducibility

Analytical calculations, explanatory redraws, and reported experimental data are explicitly separated. No new GPU benchmark, completed self-improvement experiment, or peer-review acceptance is claimed.
